NARRATOR STUDIO

Small stories. Big moments.

Plan mainly 30-second promos and short-form videos. Start with a picture, an accessible video link or an idea.

Planning and review workspace · manual production handoff. This edition saves your plans; it does not connect to production tools or start generation. Copy a brief to your own Orb when you are ready. Your connected Mac, tools and any voice/media setup are separate requirements.

How it works

  1. Create or choose a project. New projects start clean; each returning project restores its own plan.
  2. Add a reference and describe the short. Choose 15–90 seconds. Review the script and visual notes.
  3. Enter your preferred production contact below. Prepare and copy the brief, then send it yourself to your own chief-of-staff or project Orb. It can check available tools, ask for permissions and provide a review.
  4. Bring reviewed text back here. Finished files are delivered separately through Orbit attachments; this app does not download local videos or save to Photos.

Pictures are creative input. Uploaded previews are compressed working copies; keep and attach your original when needed. Video links and selected sections can only be analysed when actual media is accessible. A pasted link is not evidence that it was watched. Upload a permitted original to your Orb if access fails.

Third-party references are for research and analysis. Do not resell or redistribute without the necessary rights; research use alone does not grant permission. Explain your intended reporting/commentary use and provide actual permission or licence details. No automatic copyright clearance or access-control bypass is offered.

Still-image edits are not generated character animation. Exact image models depend on the connected tool. Physical iPhone and separate-user production setup remain unverified.
