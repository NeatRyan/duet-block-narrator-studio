How it works

This app is powered by your own dedicated production Orb. Tap Set up my production Orb, review and send the setup request. Your chief-of-staff links an existing suitable Orb or creates one under your permissions, then reports its actual model. Choose or change that model using Orbit’s Orb-chat model controls or ask your chief. An inherited default must be disclosed for your decision before production. Routine handoff to your chosen Orb does not need repeated model approval. The public app cannot create Orbs or switch models itself; it sends the explicit setup request. Tool checks report image and voice capabilities separately.

  1. Create or choose a project. New projects start clean; each returning project restores its own plan.
  2. Add a reference and describe the short. Choose 15–90 seconds. Review the script and visual notes.
  3. Choose Check tools, Draft story or Generate video. Review exactly what will be sent, then explicitly send it to your own chief-of-staff. Orbit may also ask for confirmation. Your Orb checks tools and permissions before work.
  4. Refresh request status to read a response saved by your Orb. Until a response arrives, the app cannot confirm production has started. Finished files arrive through Orbit attachments; in-app video download and Photos save are not available.

Reference-mode uploads are creative input and compressed working copies. Exact-mode uploads retain the received file bytes within the shown size limit, without AI alteration; video fitting and encoding still apply. Larger originals can be attached to your Orb. Label each person and optionally assign images to scenes. Video links and selected sections can only be analysed when actual media is accessible. A pasted link is not evidence that it was watched. Upload a permitted original to your Orb if access fails.

Third-party references are for research and analysis. Do not resell or redistribute without the necessary rights; research use alone does not grant permission. Explain your intended reporting/commentary use and provide actual permission or licence details. No automatic copyright clearance or access-control bypass is offered.

Still-image edits are not generated character animation. Exact image models depend on the connected tool. Physical iPhone and separate-user production setup remain unverified.

Existing users may need App Library → Update to install a new release. Publishing an update does not mean every installed copy has updated.
